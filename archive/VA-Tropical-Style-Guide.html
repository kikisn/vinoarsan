Usage Guidelines

  • Hero Section: Fruits positioned around edges as decorative framing elements
  • Section Dividers: Scattered fruits between major sections for visual interest
  • Backgrounds: Subtle fruit illustrations at reduced opacity as background elements
  • Cards: Small fruit icons can be used to indicate wine flavor profiles
  • Color Matching: Use fruit illustration colors for corresponding wine types
  • Size Guidelines: Keep decorative fruits between 80px-150px width for optimal visual balance

Implementation Notes

Key Design Updates (October 2025 - Tropical Theme)

  • Color Shift: From charcoal black/gold to forest green/coral with tropical fruit accents
  • Botanical Elements: Filipino fruit illustrations (bugnay, calamansi, dragon fruit, mango, mangosteen, mulberry) as decorative elements
  • Natural Aesthetic: Cream/beige backgrounds creating warm, organic feel
  • Typography: Fondamento (serif) and Noto Sans pairing for elegant yet approachable tone
  • Section Variety: Mix of light cream sections and rich forest green sections for visual rhythm
  • Decorative Patterns: Ornate geometric borders on dark sections (Limited Releases)
  • Buttons: Soft coral primary buttons with rounded corners (25px border-radius)
  • Product Cards: Clean white cards with subtle borders and green gradient placeholders
  • Interactive Elements: Carousel sliders for pairing guides and limited releases
  • Cultural Connection: Design celebrates Filipino heritage through indigenous fruit imagery

Complete Homepage Flow

Overview of how the tropical design system creates a cohesive brand experience from header to footer.

1. Header Navigation

Cream background with simple navigation links. Logo uses Fondamento serif font.

2. Hero Section

Cream background with large headline, wine bottle image, and fruit illustrations framing the content.

3. Pairing Section

Forest green background with white text. Card carousel showing wine + food pairings with images.

4. About Sections

Cream background with image + text layouts. Fruit illustrations as decorative elements.

5. Limited Releases

Black background with ornate gold borders. White text with product carousel.

6. Testimonials

Green background with decorative fruit border frame. Customer quotes in serif font.

7. Custom Orders

Forest green background with white text. Contact information and CTA for bulk orders.

8. Footer

Clean white footer with minimal links and social media icons (Google, Facebook, Instagram).
