Frequently asked questions

Where are your wines produced? ∨

All our wines are proudly handcrafted in the Philippines using fruits sourced from various provinces including Ilocos Sur, Laguna, South Cotabato, and Quezon. We support local farmers and celebrate Filipino agricultural excellence.

What makes Malibugold special? ∨

Malibugold is our award-winning semi-dry red wine made from a unique blend of Mangosteen, Lipote, and Bugnay. This distinctive combination has earned international recognition and represents the best of Filipino fruit wines.

Do you offer custom labeling for events? ∨

Yes! We specialize in custom wine bottles for corporate events, weddings, parties, and special occasions. Our beautiful fruit illustration labels can be personalized to make your event truly memorable.
