# VINO ARSAN  
**EST. 2009**  
**Proudly Handcrafted. Proudly Filipino.**

---

## 🏺 HISTORY

**Vino Arsan** began in 2008 as a humble hobby of **Mr. Arturo Olarte**, a Mechanical Engineer by profession. While working on a project site in **Nueva Ecija**, he discovered local berries — **Bugnay (Bignay)** and **Lipote** — that had color and tannin qualities similar to grapes.

Surprised that these berries had little to no commercial use, he first turned them into juice. Seeing their resemblance to red wine, he began experimenting with fermentation. Alongside his son, **Josh Olarte**, they later attended **wine-making seminars and trainings in the United States** to enhance their craft.

They started selling their wines at **weekend markets, bazaars**, and to **restaurants and companies** that appreciated their unique Filipino berry wines.

### 🏅 Achievements
To gauge their product against international standards, they joined the **2016 International Wine Making Competition** by *Wine Maker Magazine* and earned:
- 🥈 **2 Silver Medals**
- 🥉 **1 Bronze Medal**

These awards were for their **Libug (Lipote-Bugnay)** and **Malibug (Mango-Lipote-Bugnay)** berry-based red wines — out of over **2,981 entries**.

In **May 2018**, they joined the same competition again and won a:
- 🥇 **Gold Medal** for the **Malibug (Mango-Lipote-Bugnay)** Red Wine.

Following their success, they officially established **VINO ARSAN ENTERPRISES** under the **DTI**, marking their transition into a **legitimate beverage and liquor manufacturing company**.

Today, **Vino Arsan**:
- Produces and manufactures **wine, brandy, and vinegar**  
- Sells **wine-making equipment**  
- Conducts **wine-making seminars and training programs**

## 🎯 MISSION

To be the **best wine-making company in the Philippines** and to **promote the quality of Filipino-made red wine, brandy, liquor, vinegar, juice, and jams** to the world.  
To produce products with **excellence and distinction**, while supporting **local farmers** and the fruits they grow.

---

## 🌏 VISION

To create **vino** (wine) with **superior quality** and the **highest standards of health, sanitation, and cleanliness**, positioning Vino Arsan as a leading company in the Philippines and globally.

Vino Arsan aims to:
- Offer products that **create sustainable value** for both people and clients.
