Our website and Shopify checkout pages use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience and enable e-commerce functionality.

- **Essential Cookies:** Required for Buy Button functionality, shopping cart operation, and checkout process
- **Shopify Cookies:** Enables secure checkout, payment processing, and order management on vino-arsan-2.myshopify.com
- **Analytics Cookies:** Help us understand how visitors use our website and Shopify store
- **Marketing Cookies:** Facebook Pixel for tracking conversions and ad performance
- **Preference Cookies:** Remember your language and regional preferences

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect website functionality, prevent items from being added to your cart, and block your ability to complete purchases.

### Facebook Pixel
We use Facebook Pixel to:
- Measure ad campaign effectiveness
- Track conversions from Facebook ads
- Create custom audiences for targeted advertising

To opt out of Facebook tracking, visit your Facebook ad preferences or use the Global Privacy Control opt-out preference signal.

### Global Privacy Control
If you visit our website with the Global Privacy Control opt-out preference signal enabled, depending on where you are, we will automatically treat this as a request to opt-out for the device and browser that you use to visit the website. If we are able to associate the device sending the signal to a Shopify account, we will apply the opt-out request to the account as well. To learn more about Global Privacy Control, visit https://globalprivacycontrol.org/

We do not recognize other "Do Not Track" signals that may be sent from your web browser or device.
